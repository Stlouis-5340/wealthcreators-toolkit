Better together

Learn the math. Then run the math.

The playbook teaches how owners think. The calculator turns that thinking into a repeatable habit you use on every deal. Together, they're a free toolkit for your next deal.

Learn

Study the math ladder

The playbook walks gross income → vacancy → NOI → debt service → cash flow, including the three rungs most pro formas skip: vacancy, CapEx and management.

Run

Underwrite your own numbers

Plug in the real tax bill, a real insurance quote and market vacancy, not the seller's spreadsheet. The calculator runs all four metrics at once.

Judge

Check it against benchmarks

See exactly where a deal passes, needs caution or fails, and be suspicious of any deal that works on only one metric.

Repeat

Practice before you buy

The playbook's 90-day plan says to analyze 25 deals on paper before buying. Copy each result into your notes and build your buy box.
